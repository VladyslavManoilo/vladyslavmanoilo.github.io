What the app does

Vendframe helps prepare product photos with on-device background removal, backgrounds, canvas formats, shadows, exposure adjustments and optional detail enhancement. Automatic processing can make mistakes. Check edges, labels, textures and colors before publishing a product listing. You are responsible for having the rights to the photos you import and for accurately representing the items you sell.

Free and Pro

The free version includes five single-photo exports at standard quality, up to 1024 px on the longest edge. Pro includes unlimited exports, batch editing of up to 20 photos, exports up to 2048 px and optional Enhance ×2. The app shows the applicable limits and available features before purchase. A subscription does not guarantee that every input photo will process successfully.

Weekly and annual subscriptions

Pro is available as an automatically renewing weekly or annual subscription. The selected plan’s localized price and full billing period appear before purchase and in Apple’s confirmation sheet. Annual subscriptions are billed for the year upfront. The initial offers have no free trial.

Billing, renewal and cancellation

Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests are handled by Apple under its applicable policies and your statutory rights.

Restore purchases

Use Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account used for the purchase. Internet access may be required to verify or restore the subscription. Photo processing itself runs on your device.
