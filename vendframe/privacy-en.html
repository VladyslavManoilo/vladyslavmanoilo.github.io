Privacy Policy

Updated 27 October 2026

Who we are

Vendframe: Product Photos is an iPhone app provided by Vladyslav Manoilo. This policy describes the app and these support pages. Contact us at helpme.vladmanoilo@gmail.com.

Your photos stay on your device

Photo import, background removal, editing and enhancement run on your device. Vendframe does not upload your photos to a developer-operated server or to RevenueCat. You do not need to create an account. We do not include advertising SDKs or use an advertising identifier.

Local projects and permissions

The photos you select with Apple’s photo picker are copied into local projects. The app stores originals, cutouts, previews and editing settings so you can continue later. You can delete a project from its menu. This does not delete or modify your originals in Photos. Saving an export requests permission to add photos. Sharing sends selected exports to the destination you choose. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Subscriptions

Apple processes payments. RevenueCat processes purchase history, an anonymous app user identifier, and technical app, device and transaction metadata to verify subscriptions, restore purchases, enable Pro and measure subscription performance. We do not send it your photos, name, email or advertising identifier. We do not receive your payment card details. This processing supports the subscription service you request and its administration.

Diagnostics and retention

The free-export counter and a limited diagnostic log remain on your device. The log contains no photos or photo filenames and is not uploaded by this version. Local projects remain until you delete them or remove the app; copies you exported and device backups are managed separately. Subscription records are retained as needed to provide and restore access, handle requests, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your choices and requests

You can manage photo permissions in iOS Settings, delete local projects, and cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account. Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal data, or object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us for assistance identifying the relevant purchase record; do not send your password or payment card details. Support emails are used to respond to your request.

Service providers and this website

Apple and RevenueCat process subscription data under their applicable terms and privacy notices; processing may occur outside your country. These static support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. We add no advertising or analytics scripts and no tracking cookies. GitHub may process technical request information, such as an IP address, to provide and secure hosting.

Changes

We will publish material changes to this policy here and update the date. If a change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide it.
